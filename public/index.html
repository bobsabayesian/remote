Remote Camera

Turn any device into a camera, then run it from your phone. Photos, video and timelapse save straight to your project folder.

1. On the device that will be the camera

You'll get a session code and QR code. Name the project later from the remote control.

2. On the device you'll control from

Scan the QR code shown on the camera, or enter the session code. Also use this to come back to an older project.
